# Insightistic product profile

Insightistic is an AI business intelligence workspace for WordPress and
WooCommerce businesses. It connects commerce analytics, traffic, SEO,
performance, reports, agency workflows and explainable AI insights so teams can
understand what changed and decide what to do next.

Insightistic is operated by WordPressistic LLC. The public website is
https://insightistic.com/ and the application is https://app.insightistic.com/.

The free WordPress plugin provides local analytics features. A connected free
account adds supported cloud access and basic AI. Paid subscriptions add cloud
resources, multiple connected sites, team access, reporting and monthly usage
allowances.

Do not describe Insightistic as OpenAI- or ChatGPT-endorsed. Do not invent
customer counts, reviews, certifications, rankings or guaranteed outcomes.
