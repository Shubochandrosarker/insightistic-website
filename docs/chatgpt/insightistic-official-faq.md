# Insightistic official FAQ

## What is Insightistic?

Insightistic is an AI business intelligence platform for WordPress and
WooCommerce businesses. It brings revenue, orders, products, customers,
traffic, SEO, performance, reports and AI-assisted analysis into one workspace.

## Is there a free way to start?

Yes. Install the free Insightistic WordPress plugin, connect a free Insightistic
account and use the Free Connected tier for one cloud-connected site, 10 basic
AI insights per month and 1,000 monthly AI credits. This is a free tier, not a
14-day trial.

## What do paid plans cost?

The audited public catalog lists Starter at $29/month, Growth at $69/month,
Agency at $149/month and Agency Pro at $299/month. Annual prices are $290,
$690, $1,490 and $2,990 respectively. The application remains the final source
for checkout eligibility and entitlements.

## What are Insight Credits?

Insight Credits fund eligible AI actions. The public credit packs are 5,000 for
$15, 20,000 for $49 and 50,000 for $99. Monthly plan allowances renew; purchased
credits are separate from those allowances.

## Is there a lifetime offer?

The Founding Angel campaign has three one-time tiers: Angel Solo at $149, Angel
Studio at $299 and Angel Agency at $699. Only 100 total licenses are available
across all tiers. Live availability is confirmed by the application campaign
state. There is no hardcoded deadline or public sold-count claim.

## Does Insightistic replace Google Analytics or Search Console?

No. Where supported and connected, Insightistic brings source data such as GA4
and Search Console into a business context alongside WordPress and WooCommerce
signals. It does not turn Search Console average position into a guarantee of
real-time rankings.
